Review hours = projects × submittals × (1 + resubmittal rate) × minutes ÷ 60.
Labor savings = hours × % saved × (salary × overhead ÷ working hours).
Rework avoided = projects × errors caught × cost per error.
Schedule avoided = projects × days saved × cost per day. Cost per day = price × 8% ÷ (duration × 260 working days).
Projects per year = PEs ÷ duration. Annual savings = labor + rework + schedule. Assumes one PE owns each project.
